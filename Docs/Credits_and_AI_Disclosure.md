## What is not in the game

- No real passport, ID number, machine-readable zone, crest or official insignia. The Festival of Passports, its booklet and every character are fictional.
- No partner or sponsor branding (no Abu Dhabi Gaming or Unity logos) and no claim of endorsement.
- No sacred symbols or religious items are used in the Passenger's disguises.
- No gore. The Passenger never touches the player. Its fingers stop short at the near miss.

## Claims this project does not make

- No awards, sponsorship or partnership.
- No playtest by anyone other than the automated in-build tests listed in `Docs/QA/QA_Report.md`. Headset play has not been verified by a person (see that report).
